Label-free anomaly detection

Current ECG workflows rely on slow manual screening and supervised AI that needs labelled examples, so rare cardiac anomalies are missed.

CardioAI learns what a healthy heartbeat looks like, reconstructs each incoming beat from that baseline, and flags any beat the baseline cannot explain. No examples of the disease are needed.
